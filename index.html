About me

JavaMicroservicesREST APIs Cloud & ContainersTesting & MonitoringMachine Learning

I am a backend software developer and a graduate student in the M.S. in AI and Data Analytics program at Indiana Wesleyan University.

In my daily work, I design and build Java-based microservices and REST APIs that run in the cloud. I work with databases, containerized deployments, and monitoring tools, and I collaborate with architects, QA engineers, and business stakeholders to turn requirements into reliable software.

I started my graduate program because AI is changing how software is built and used. I want to do more than use AI tools. I want to understand how they work, test them carefully, and help teams adopt them responsibly.

My goal is to grow into a role where I build AI-powered features and help organizations move from AI experiments to dependable, trustworthy systems. This portfolio documents that journey, one project at a time.
